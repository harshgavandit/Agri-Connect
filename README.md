# AgriConnect

AgriConnect is an agricultural marketplace where farmers and sellers list products, and buyers browse, compare, and order them. It also brings together local seller discovery, delivery tools, agricultural resources, and community sharing.

[Visit AgriConnect](https://agri-connect-flame.vercel.app)

## Buying and selling

- Browse produce, groceries, livestock, farming inputs, tools, and other agricultural products by category.
- Search products, compare listings, save favourites, and view seller profiles.
- Find nearby sellers and listings on the map.
- Add products to a cart, choose a delivery address, review the price breakdown, and place an order.
- View order details and payment status from your account.
- Create product listings and manage incoming orders through the seller dashboard.

Stripe and Razorpay checkout are currently configured for test payments.

## More in the app

- **AI assistant:** Ask questions, search for products, translate text, and use voice commands to navigate the marketplace.
- **Logistics:** View shipping estimates, shipment tracking, and delivery collaboration options.
- **Land:** Browse agricultural land listings.
- **Share & Care:** Browse and post community sharing listings.
- **Farmer resources:** Explore farming information, government schemes, and agricultural technology resources.
- **Student portal:** Access student resources and support requests.
- **Language and accessibility settings:** Change the interface language and adjust accessibility preferences.

## Administration

The control centre gives authorised staff tools to manage users, sellers, product listings, categories, orders, and verification requests. Staff access is controlled through roles and permissions, with audit records for administrative activity.
